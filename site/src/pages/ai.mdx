**Last Modified:** November 14, 2026

I still do all of my writing myself. Since I started writing technically in 2014, I've never been comfortable having
someone else write or speak on my behalf. What has changed is my view on AI's trustworthiness. Early on, I didn't think
it was reliable enough to lean on heavily. Now that I can get agents to return the sources they're drawing on, I have a
lot more confidence in where the information is coming from and when to push back on it.

### Technology

My setup has shifted a lot since I last updated this page. [Claude][] has become my primary assistant for most
day-to-day work. For anything I want to iterate on privately or offline, I self-host a [gemma4:26b][] model via
[Ollama][]. A recent upgrade to a gaming rig has made local hosting significantly more practical than it used to be.

For self-hosted model access, I've moved away from [Cline][] as my driver. I now use GoModel as a gateway, with
[ZooCode][] and [pi.dev][] as my primary clients depending on what I'm working on.

[Claude]: https://claude.ai/

[Ollama]: https://ollama.com/

### Writing & Editing

I use AI to help edit my own writing, but never to generate it. This includes:

- Exploring alternative wordings for sentences to reduce complexity
- Iterating on statements to improve clarity and strength
- Identifying problematic areas that may need further detail
- Turning text into speech when proofreading articles

<br />

### Research

I've started leaning on Claude for spot research. It's a fast way to get oriented on a topic, but I verify everything
along the way. AI gets things wrong with a lot of confidence, so I treat its output as a starting point and confirm
sources and facts before acting on them.

### Development & Prototyping

I use it for rapid prototyping, writing automation scripts, generating boilerplate, and getting a second read on code
I've written. Whatever it produces, I read it, understand it, and make sure it actually fits before it goes anywhere.

### My Philosophy

I see AI as something that speeds up parts of my work, not something that does my work for me. The decisions,
implementations, and all written content are mine. That accountability doesn't go away just because a model was involved
somewhere in the process.
